My love, julie.

Sometimes the beginning of everything is just a food park on an ordinary day.

Unbeknownst to me, that ordinary afternoon quietly drafted the first page of a story I never intended to write. You were just another stranger occupying the same corner of the world as I was, but something tugged at me—had me looking back for you that day. Life apparently disguised permanence as coincidence. If I’m being fair to our story, the beginning wasn’t perfect. We weren’t one of those stories where everything aligned, where timing cooperated, and misunderstandings politely excused themselves. There were missed timings, disappearances, unnecessary issues; most of which, admittedly, carried my fingerprints. Yet somehow, despite every inconvenience I unintentionally became, you stayed. People often talk about loud gestures as the greatest expression of love, but I think consistency is far rarer. Anyone can love loudly for a day. You loved patiently across days I made difficult.

I wanted to tell you all of this while sitting beside you, because voices carry things letters cannot. But until distance grows tired of separating us, these words will have to travel where I cannot. I only hope that when they finally reach you, they carry more than text and language. I know that if I asked, you’d find a way to come, because that’s the kind of person you are. But today isn’t about overcoming distance for my sake. I hope you spend it laughing until your cheeks hurt, smiling without restraint, and allowing yourself to be celebrated; not because you have to earn it, but because you already deserve it simply by being yourself. You’ve become something strange in my life—not strange in a bad way, but in the way gravity is strange. We don’t usually notice gravity until we imagine living without it. That’s what you’ve become to me. Not a constant source of excitement that fades once wonder disappears, but a quiet certainty that has woven itself into my everyday routine.

Communication has never been my strongest language. Words can fail. They can arrive too late or too early. They can be interpreted differently from how they were meant. But loving you has made me ambitious enough to become bilingual. If my gestures have always spoken louder than my sentences, then let this letter be my attempt at translating them. Every hug I’ve held onto a little longer. Every glance I’ve stolen when you weren’t looking. Every time I found myself smiling because you were simply being yourself. They have all been saying the same thing: I love you.

May you never confuse kindness with weakness. They’re often mistaken for one another, but they’re worlds apart. Glass shatters because it’s fragile; flowers bloom through cracks in concrete because they’re stubborn enough to chase the sun anyway. I’ve always thought you resembled the latter more than the former. Somehow, you leave little fingerprints on moments that should’ve been forgettable, and months later they still find their way back into people’s minds. I hope life never convinces you that you have to earn the right to take up space. You don’t have to trim your laughter until it’s polite or compress your excitement until it’s digestible. Some people were born to whisper; you, I think, were made to echo. What a terrible waste it would be if you ever decided to become quiet simply because someone else was uncomfortable with joy that honest.

You’re special to me, Julie. That sentence looks deceptively simple on text, but it carries more weight than language can comfortably hold. And unless the day comes when you tell me to let go, I will continue choosing you with the same certainty I did yesterday and the same certainty I hope to carry tomorrow.

Should the stars ever rearrange themselves, I hope they still remember to draw a path that leads me to you.

With all my heart,

sometimes dinosaur, most of the time alien.
